# open_STRD1806

Open hardware reconstruction of the Sanken STR-D1806 hybrid IC for SMPS (Switch Mode Power Supplies).

## 🚨 Disclaimer & Safety Warning
* **Status:** This design is **untested** (Alpha), but mostly follows the original circuit design of the STR-D1806.
* **Warranty:** The schematic, PCB design, and manufacturing files are provided **"as is" without any warranty or guarantee of function**.
* **Risk:** High voltage is dangerous. The use, building, and testing of this design is entirely **at your own risk**. 

## ⚡ Technical Specification
The circuit board is designed to work in switch-mode power supplies with input voltages up to **240V AC RMS** (rectified to approximately **340V DC** on the primary rail). 

The PCB is designed as a single-sided board, optimized for **Aluminum Substrate / IMS (Insulated Metal Substrate) manufacturing** to ensure optimal thermal dissipation to the original heatsink of the PSU.
The PCB accepts either **D2PAK(TO-263)** or **TO-220** packages for the surface mounted **Power NPN Transistor**
